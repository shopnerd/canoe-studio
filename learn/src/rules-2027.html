Guide · 2026–27 ASCE Concrete Canoe Competition

The 2027 rules, explained

The rules that shape the hull, the mix, the mold and the display, paraphrased in plain English with the section number for each. This is a study aid. The official RFP and posted RFI answers always win.

The theme this year is “Simplify, Collaborate, Celebrate” (the 40th competition). The biggest changes: molds must be built by hand with no CNC, the report is now a design narrative plus construction drawings, and the men’s and women’s slaloms became a single co-ed slalom. Finals are at Kansas State University, June 17–19, 2027.

The hull

Thickness · §6.1.1 & §6.9

At Finals the hull wall can be at most ½ in, with a ⅛ in tolerance, measured with calipers wherever the judges choose. Ribs and gunwales don’t count. The canoe is weighed and measured against what the Design Narrative reported. App: Rules tab, “Hull thickness”.

Freeboard for the names · §6.2.2 & §6.7.1

With all four co-ed sprint paddlers aboard, the school and canoe names must stay fully above water. The letters are 5 in ± ¼ in tall, and their tops sit 1 in below the gunwale, so the gunwale needs about 6¼ in of freeboard wherever the names go. Breaking it costs 30 seconds on the co-ed sprint, and the calculation must appear in the Calculation Package. App: freeboard chart on the Rules tab, with the run of gunwale that clears 6¼ in.

Decorations · §6.2.3 & §6.7

No decorative 3D elements above the gunwale line. Names go on both sides, at least five characters. Integral pigments must meet ASTM C979. Stains must be penetrating, non-film-forming and ≤ 350 g/L VOC, with at most two coats, and can’t hide the concrete texture. Sealers are clear only (silane/siloxane ≤ 350 g/L, or ASTM C1315), at most two coats. No glitter or particles.

Flotation · §6.6

Filled with water, the canoe must float roughly level near the surface within 2 minutes. Any flotation foam has to be within 3 ft of the bow and stern tips and encased in concrete. Failing the first attempt is a deduction, and foam added at a Symposium comes out again for Finals. App: swamp test plus the hollow volume available in the end zones.

The mold · §6.2.1

Teams build their own molds. No outsourcing, and no CNC processes: mills, routers, laser cutters, water jets and 3D printers, “but not limited to” those. CNC is allowed for jigs and templates that help build the mold by hand. Old molds can only be reused with proof they were hand-built.

How we read it

Laser-cut templates with foam cut by a hand-guided hot wire should be fine: the machine only made the templates. A CNC hot-wire cutter is a CNC process making the mold, so it is very likely not allowed. Several 2025 finalists used 3D-printed or CNC-routed mold parts that won’t pass this year. Confirm the team’s exact process with an RFI before Jan 25, 2027. App: section and hot-wire template exports.

Calculations the report needs · §5.7.1

Up to four pages of step-by-step example calculations, done digitally, covering:

  • Hull freeboard under four-person loading
  • 2D structural analysis for the 3-person co-ed endurance slalom load case: maximum moment and shear, with and without factors of safety or load factors
  • Moment of inertia (section properties hand-calculated; exact CAD values aren’t accepted)
  • Maximum stresses
  • Hull punching shear per ACI

App: the Rules tab computes all of these from the exact geometry. Use it to check the hand calculations, not to replace them.

The display · §7

  • Display fits a 4 × 8 × 7 ft space, judged from the front, self-supporting. No electronics, lights, sound, sponsor items or scents.
  • Cross-section: full scale, showing raw and finished stages and the mold, every element labeled, off the ground, in its own 4 × 4 × 7 ft space.
  • Canoe on stands about 4 ft to the top of the gunwale, underside visible.
  • An optional elevator pitch. Judges have under 10 minutes for everything, and the pitch comes out of that time.
  • Required samples and documents: see the checklist in Lessons from the 2025 Finals.

Races · §9

  • Symposia: co-ed 200 m slalom (2 paddlers), women’s and men’s 200 m sprints (2 each), co-ed 200 m sprint (4).
  • Finals: co-ed 600 m endurance slalom (3), women’s and men’s 200 m sprints, co-ed 200 m heats and a 400 m final (4).
  • Seats ≤ 20 × 20 × 20 in; mats ≤ 20 × 30 × ½ in; no straps or anything attaching a paddler to the canoe; Coast Guard–approved life jackets at all times (Exhibit 8).
