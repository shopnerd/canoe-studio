Canoe Studio

A browser version of canoe_script_012226.gh, the Grasshopper definition for parametrically designing the concrete canoe. Same parameters, same construction:

  1. Curve generation. Four control curves — midship cross section, bow/keel profile, plan (gunwale) and sheer (top rocker) — each interpolated through 3–5 points you place with setbacks, and validated against its bounding box.
  2. Canoe lofting. Stations from midship to the forefoot tween the cross section into the stem, sit on the keel rocker, swing from 0° to 90° about the keel, and scale so their width at the gunwale matches the plan curve. The loft is mirrored to all four quadrants.
  3. Final shaping. Trim to the sheer line, optional stern chop, and a concrete shell at the wall thickness.
  4. Mold blocks. A block array trimmed flat at depth + floor, with the canoe cut out — ready to print or mill.

The Rules 2027 tab checks the design against the 2026–27 ASCE Concrete Canoe RFP (issued Sept 8, 2026): hull thickness, four-paddler freeboard for the names, flotation test and foam zones, concrete unit weight, reinforcement, and the 3-person structural load case.

Added beyond the script: hydrostatics (draft, freeboard, form coefficients, metacentric height), a righting-arm curve, swamp flotation check, friction drag, section templates, offsets table and STL export.

Analysis is an early-design estimate — verify with a float test. Units are inches and pounds. Geometry kernel: Manifold (Apache-2.0). Viewer: three.js (MIT).
